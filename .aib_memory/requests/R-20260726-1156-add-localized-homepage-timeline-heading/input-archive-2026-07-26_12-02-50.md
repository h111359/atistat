## Input

The timeline below "ЗНАКОВИ ПРОЕКТИ" needs own title with size of the one for ЗНАКОВИ ПРОЕКТИ. Make such

### Goal

Add a distinct localized heading for the homepage timeline so visitors can clearly distinguish the complete company-and-project chronology from the preceding Flagship Projects subsection.

### Stakeholders and user need

- Bulgarian- and English-speaking website visitors are the directly affected users.
- Visitors currently encounter the timeline immediately after the Flagship Projects cards without a dedicated heading, which weakens the visual and semantic hierarchy.
- The change benefits all localized homepage variants and has no additional impacted parties.

### Business value and priority

- Improve content scanning, section comprehension, and consistency within the Experience area.
- Treat this as a current-iteration UI correction because the missing title affects every page containing the duplicated timeline.
- No existing heading specifically labels the complete timeline.

### Scope

- Add the Bulgarian title `ХРОНОЛОГИЯ` to:
  - `index.html`
  - `index-bg.html`
  - `index.html?lang=bg.html`
- Add the English title `TIMELINE` to:
  - `index-en.html`
  - `index.html?lang=en.html`
  - `opit/index.html`
- Place the title immediately after the complete Flagship Projects section and before the entire timeline component, including its selected-milestone panel.
- Give the title the complete visual treatment used by `ЗНАКОВИ ПРОЕКТИ / FLAGSHIP PROJECTS`: matching responsive font size, green color, uppercase presentation, letter spacing, and line height.
- Preserve appropriate spacing between the Flagship Projects section, the new title, and the timeline.
- Use semantic heading markup consistent with the existing Experience section hierarchy and associate the timeline component with its localized heading for accessibility.
- Keep the two shared stylesheet copies byte-identical if shared CSS changes are required.
- Extend the static test coverage to verify title localization, source order, semantic association, and parity across all six timeline documents.
- Update `.aib_memory/context.md` to record the new localized timeline heading behavior.

### Out of scope

- Do not change timeline milestones, project data, cards, selected panels, galleries, navigation behavior, or JavaScript interactions.
- Do not change the content or design of the Flagship Projects cards.
- Do not modify project-detail pages or homepage sections outside the Experience area.

### Constraints and assumptions

- Preserve the static, dependency-free architecture and existing bilingual route model.
- Preserve keyboard interaction, accessible timeline state, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Treat visual equivalence as matching all agreed heading properties, not font size alone.
- Apply the change consistently to all six duplicated timeline documents to prevent localized route drift.
- Assume the existing `ЗНАКОВИ ПРОЕКТИ / FLAGSHIP PROJECTS` title is the visual source of truth.

### Success criteria

- Each of the three Bulgarian timeline documents contains exactly one visible `ХРОНОЛОГИЯ` heading.
- Each of the three English timeline documents contains exactly one visible `TIMELINE` heading.
- In every document, the new heading occurs after the closing Flagship Projects section and before the opening timeline component.
- The heading semantically labels the timeline and follows the existing Experience heading hierarchy.
- The new heading matches the complete visual style of the Flagship Projects heading at desktop and responsive widths.
- Existing timeline content and behavior remain unchanged.
- Automated static tests verify localization, placement, accessibility association, and six-document parity.
- Both shared stylesheet copies remain byte-identical.
