## Overview

Request ID: R-20260725-1900

Request title: Update Viber icon, timeline, and English sections

### Background

The developer reports that the current Viber artwork does not resemble the official Viber icon, that the selected-project gallery is disconnected from the Experience timeline, and that the English Why ATISTAT and Questions and Answers sections do not match the Bulgarian source-of-truth presentation and content.

### Scope

The request covers the Viber controls on the static homepage family; the desktop and mobile Experience timeline; the seven selected-project gallery sets and their native dialog; new stylized timeline artwork derived from the first gallery photograph for gallery-only projects; shared timeline, gallery, modal, and localized-section CSS and JavaScript; and English translations of the Bulgarian Why ATISTAT and FAQ sections.

### Out of scope

Dedicated project-detail routes, changes to the underlying business claims in the Bulgarian Why ATISTAT or FAQ copy, replacement of the submitted full-resolution gallery photographs, introduction of a third-party front-end library or build pipeline, and general redesign of unrelated homepage sections are excluded.

## Input Interpretation

### User Original Inputs

- Replace the current Viber artwork with an official Viber icon found on the Internet.
- Connect the selected-project gallery to the Experience timeline by merging the project sets into the timeline, adding a small clickable image compilation for projects with galleries, opening the respective project's images in a modal, and generating a timeline-style image from the first modal photograph for projects that are not already on the timeline.
- Make the English Why ATISTAT section match the style and translated content of the Bulgarian ЗАЩО АТИСТАТ source of truth.
- Make the English Questions and Answers section match the style and translated content of the Bulgarian ВЪПРОСИ И ОТГОВОРИ source of truth.

### AI interpretation

The static homepage experience is to expose one coherent project record per timeline project across desktop and mobile layouts. A record that owns a submitted gallery must include an explicit, keyboard-operable gallery affordance that opens a project-scoped native dialog, while timeline-only projects remain available without an invented gallery. The union currently consists of ten unique projects: EOS Matrix, Montekanal, EMA, Elemag, UBB Interlease, British School of Sofia, Apartment Arcadia, Apartment Louis Ayer, Bebelan Project, and Power Properties. The three company milestones—Correct Project, Engineering Systems, and ATISTAT—remain a distinct milestone type unless the developer chooses a narrower merge model.

The four gallery-only projects require new monochrome architectural line-art timeline assets based on their first submitted photographs. Their chronology and descriptive metadata must not be invented because no year, service classification, or partner attribution is present in the workspace. The Viber replacement must use an unaltered asset from Viber's official Brand Center and must be applied consistently to the Viber controls selected by the developer. The English Why and FAQ sections must be structurally equivalent to the Bulgarian sections and contain faithful English translations rather than independent English marketing variants.

Applicability: keep the modal photographs lazy-loaded, give every image intrinsic dimensions, and use generated lightweight timeline/compilation assets rather than forcing the 52.5 MiB gallery into the timeline's initial render path.
