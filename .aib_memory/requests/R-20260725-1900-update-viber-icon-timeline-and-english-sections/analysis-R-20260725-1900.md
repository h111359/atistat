## Overview

Request title: Update Viber icon, timeline, and English sections

### Background

The developer reports that the current Viber artwork does not resemble the official Viber icon, that the selected-project gallery is disconnected from the Experience timeline, and that the English Why ATISTAT and Questions and Answers sections do not match the Bulgarian source-of-truth presentation and content.

### Scope

The request covers six static homepage documents (five root homepage routes and opit/index.html); the desktop and mobile Experience timeline; the seven selected-project gallery sets and their native dialog; new stylized timeline artwork derived from the first gallery photograph for gallery-only projects; shared timeline, gallery, modal, and localized-section CSS and JavaScript; and English translations of the Bulgarian Why ATISTAT and FAQ sections.

### Out of scope

Dedicated project-detail routes, changes to the underlying business claims in the Bulgarian Why ATISTAT or FAQ copy, replacement of the submitted full-resolution gallery photographs, introduction of a third-party front-end library or build pipeline, and general redesign of unrelated homepage sections are excluded.
